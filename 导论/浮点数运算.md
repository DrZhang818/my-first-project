1. 基础对阶加法

   计算 $(1.101 \times 2^3) + (1.011 \times 2^1)$

   > $$
   > \begin{aligned}
   > \text{原式 }&= (1.101 + 0.01011) \times 2^3\\
   > &= 1.11111 \times 2^3
   > \end{aligned}
   > $$

2. 

   计算 $(1.101 \times 2^5) + (1.011 \times 2^5)$

   >$$
   >\begin{aligned}
   >\text{原式 }&= (1.101 + 1.011) \times 2^5\\
   >&= 11.000 \times 2^5\\
   >&= 1.1 \times 2^6
   >\end{aligned}
   >$$

3. 计算 $(1.000 \times 2^4) - (0.111 \times 2^4) $

   >$$
   >\begin{aligned}
   >\text{原式 }&= (1.000 - 0.111) \times 2^4\\
   >&= 0.001 \times 2^4\\
   >&= 1.000 \times 2^1
   >\end{aligned}
   >$$
